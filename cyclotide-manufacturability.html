The 1-in-15 Problem

Peptides with 6 cysteines can form 15 different disulfide bond combinations (isomers). Usually, only one is the biologically active "cystine knot".

Unoptimized sequences fold randomly, capping yield at ~6.6%, driving raw synthesis costs to $80,000+/gram. Our dWJS optimized sequences thermodynamically favor the native knot, pushing yield above 80% and dropping costs exponentially.
